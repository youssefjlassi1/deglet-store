Notre Histoire

Depuis des générations, la famille Dhaoui cultive avec passion les meilleures dattes Deglet Nour dans les oasis du sud tunisien. Notre engagement envers l'excellence et la tradition fait de chaque datte un trésor du désert tunisien.

Why Choose Us

Frigorifique of South was established in 2000 by the Dhaoui family, capitalizing on more than 19 years of expertise in the sourcing and trading of dates. Today, with over 7 000 tons of dates shipped to markets around the world every year, the company operates state-of-the-art, hygienic facilities and a highly skilled team dedicated to packing premium natural Deglet Nour dates in full compliance with international standards. Our product portfolio includes Dates on the Branch, Standard Dates and Processed Dates.

Factory

Our conditioning facility is equipped with modern, high-performance machinery, including multiple grading tables and automated packaging lines, ensuring consistently excellent quality dates.

Upon arrival, the dates are carefully disinfected, sorted and then stored in temperature-controlled cold rooms.

Each year, we process thousands of tons of dates that are exported to numerous countries worldwide.

  • Storage Capacity: 5 500 Tons
